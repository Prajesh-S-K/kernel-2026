Wokwi diagnostic with ONE four-pin momentary tactile pushbutton (wokwi-pushbutton-6mm, GPIO4 <-> GND).
Observed in the Wokwi web editor (no sign-in, nothing saved or shared). Wokwi simulates contact bounce
by default (the part's `bounce` attribute was left at its default). The button was pressed and released
by dispatching mouse events on the part, with holds of 1.5-5 s of simulation time.
The raw state ("button PRESSED / released") and the latched permission ("control ENABLED / DISABLED")
are separate fields on every serial line; `toggles` counts accepted toggles.

Boot with the button HELD:
  CONTROL DISABLED
  Button is HELD at boot: release it, then press once to enable.
  ... button PRESSED | control DISABLED | toggles=0 | ok=16 fail=0      (held for several seconds: nothing)
Released (no press yet):
  ... button released | control DISABLED | toggles=0 | ok=201 fail=0   (release alone enables nothing)
First press, held 2 s:
  CONTROL ENABLED (latched; control is NOT resumed by this)
  ... button PRESSED | control ENABLED | toggles=1 | ok=211 fail=0
Still held, 5 s in total:
  ... button PRESSED | control ENABLED | toggles=1 | ok=226 fail=0      (a long hold toggles once)
Released:
  ... button released | control ENABLED | toggles=1 | ok=236 fail=0    (permission outlives the press)
Second press:
  CONTROL DISABLED
  ... button PRESSED | control DISABLED | toggles=2 | ok=246 fail=0
Released, then a third press and release:
  ... button released | control DISABLED | toggles=2 | ok=256 fail=0
  CONTROL ENABLED (latched; control is NOT resumed by this)
  ... button released | control ENABLED | toggles=3 | ok=271 fail=0

Every accepted press toggled exactly once (toggles went 0,1,1,1,2,2,3). This is a simulation of an
idealised part: it says nothing about a real tactile switch's bounce, contact resistance, wiring or
which pins of the real component form a contact pair (continuity testing decides that).
